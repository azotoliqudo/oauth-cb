Privacy Policy

bot ds is a personal, self-hosted assistant used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app accesses the owner's own Google Mail account (IMAP/SMTP) to list, read, mark and draft emails for the owner.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens and email data are stored only on the owner's private home server. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
